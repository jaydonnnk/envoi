The same four steps, in words

This section is what reduced-motion and no-JavaScript visitors get instead of the scroll film. Nothing is lost.

Step 1

Scan

Open Envoi and scan the sticker. It identifies the shop. It is never a payment code, and only enrolled shops can be paid.

Step 2

Ask

AI explains unfamiliar dishes and builds your order within your budget. Prices come only from the shop's menu.

Step 3

Approve

You see the total and who gets paid, then approve in PayPal. The money goes to the shop's own PayPal.

Step 4

Show the stall

After Paid, show your order in the stall's language, so they know what to make.

1. Project in brief (01-product.md)
* Envoi is a PayPal AI hackathon web app. A visitor buys in person from a shop enrolled with Envoi, paying with PayPal (or, at Singapore shops, a PYUSD devnet wallet); money goes straight to the shop's own PayPal account. A buyer can type one sentence, scan the shop's sticker, or get a one-time review link from an AI agent (Ask Envoi in the site, or the terminal reference agent); the human always approves the exact payment.
* Buyer: "I am visiting Singapore. I want to buy something from this shop, but I do not have a payment method it accepts. I need to know whether I can pay, what I am approving, and whether the payment actually reached the shop."
* Merchant: "I want visitors to find my shop and pay using a method I have chosen to accept, without sending the money through another company's account."
* Success target 4 of 5 first-time buyers stays proposed and is NEVER measured; no usability, completion, timing, adoption or yield claim.
* Hard rules: test money only (PayPal sandbox, devnet); demo shops labeled FIXTURE; Envoi is never the payee; no AI sets a price; agents propose and humans approve every payment; the assistant cannot pay, approve or read a receipt; pending, failed or uncertain is never shown as paid.

Already have your menu online?
Start from your page.

Paste the link to your shop's own menu page. Envoi reads the text on that page once and drafts your items. You check every price in the table, then confirm the menu yourself. Nothing is published by the draft.

Type the menu instead

Public pages only. Envoi does not sign in, follow links or read pictures on the page. Photo import is still not available.

What stays the same

  1. The draft is never published by itself; you confirm the whole menu.
  2. A price appears only if those exact digits are printed as text on your page.
  3. An item name that is not on your page is left out.
  4. Envoi keeps no copy of your page after the draft returns.
  5. Editing later needs your confirmation again.

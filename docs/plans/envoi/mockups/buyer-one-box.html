What stays the same

  1. Only registered shops and published, available items can enter a basket.
  2. Prices come from the shop's menu, never from the AI.
  3. Envoi prices the basket again on the review.
  4. You approve in PayPal sandbox or your wallet yourself.
  5. Scanning the sticker still identifies the shop only.

Iced lemon tea and Iced milk tea are sample items for this mockup; Test Shop's live menu does not have them yet.
